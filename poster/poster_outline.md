# Poster Outline

## Problem Statement

Financial forecasting inference can be computationally expensive on low-cost hardware.

## Objective

Benchmark memory-efficient inference techniques for financial time-series forecasting.

## Methodology

Describe datasets, forecasting task, models, hardware, strict experiment matrix, cleanup policy, and tracking setup.

## Experiments

Show model/backend/precision/batch-size matrix with latency, memory, throughput, and accuracy metrics.

## Results

Summarize VRAM reduction, latency reduction, throughput changes, and accuracy tradeoffs.

## Conclusion

Recommend a deployment strategy for low-cost financial AI inference based on measured evidence.
